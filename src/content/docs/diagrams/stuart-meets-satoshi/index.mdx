Stuart and Satoshi are peers in a p2p messaging channel, similar to Signal. A `did:btcr2` DID identifies each peer.
Stuart rotates a key. Satoshi resolves the DID of Stuart and verifies the new key before Satoshi trusts a message.

In the terms of the specification, Stuart is the DID controller and Satoshi is a relying party. The diagrams do not
show one implementation. Each diagram links to its Mermaid source file.
